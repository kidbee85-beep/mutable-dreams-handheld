MUTABLE DREAMS ON THE MIYOO MINI (OnionOS 4.3 or later)

Install: copy the folders Roms/ and App/ of this zip onto the root of the Onion card (they join the existing ones;
nothing there is replaced). Mutable Dreams then appears under Games > Ports and under Apps.

The screen shows the Brick's stage made 640 x 480, at 15 pictures a second (the Cortex-A7 keeps its strength for the
sound). The Miyoo has MENU but no F1, F2 or FN:
  L2 (tap)      the quick bar: garden, mixer, Sound Lab, atlas, sequence, dream map, worlds, archive, modulation,
                voice, space, help, MENU and PLAY MODE (instead of FN)
  MENU          the jump menu       SELECT (hold) the button map
  The quick bar's last stones: LIGHT PICTURE, CALM MOTION and DEUTSCH / ENGLISH (the whole interface in German).
New since the first handheld build (0.60): the PLAYGROUND (ten toys and little games: bounce, orbits, rain, swing,
firefly, wheel, echo, seed, jelly, starfall), DREAM TALES, THE GUIDE, 84 WORLDS, German and English, the musical
garden. The manual (German and English, with pictures) comes beside this package as PDF.
No WiFi: the ARCHIVE cannot fetch; the voices already in samples/voices play.
The Sound Lab with every module at once is too much for the Miyoo's two cores: use a few modules at a time.

If something does not work, Roms/PORTS/Games/MutableDreams/log.txt says what happened. Files that change the start:
  keytest.on   every button pressed is written to log.txt
  selftest.on  plays 30 s by itself and writes its measurements (frame rate, sound) to log.txt
  calm.on      CALM MOTION from the start (lighter for the two cores)
  full.on      the full picture instead of the light one
  nofast.on    the CPU governor left alone (else "performance" while it runs, the old one put back after; no overclock)
  scaleone.on  the picture made smaller on one core only (to compare)
  burst.off    no sound made ahead between frames (to compare)
  sidefull.on  the columns beside the stage (tracks, dials, the top line and the hints) drawn in every frame;
               without it, in the light picture they are drawn every other frame and copied between (1.5.8)
MIYOO MINI FLIP: this same package (Onion 4.5 or newer). The title says MINI FLIP. The Flip has no headphone jack:
  put the USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter it came with into its USB-C port. The sound goes the way every Onion game's does;
  if it stays on the speaker, log.txt has a "usb-c audio" part at the start and the end - please bring it back.
  The Flip asks for 20 frames a second (the Mini 15); fps15.on: 15 there too.
Bundled: the Miyoo SDL2 (mmiyoo, by steward-fu, as TrackWerk uses it) and its NEON helper, in libs/.
