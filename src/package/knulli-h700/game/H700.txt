MUTABLE DREAMS ON THE ANBERNIC H700 HANDHELDS (Knulli) - by Kid Bunka

For the Allwinner H700 handhelds with Knulli: RG35XX (Plus, H, SP, 2024), RG40XX (H, V), RG CubeXX, RG34XX.
A community test: it is the Trimui Brick's program (the same aarch64 build, Knulli's own SDL2), not yet run on an
H700 by us. Please send log.txt back if something does not work.

INSTALL
  Unpack the zip and copy "Mutable Dreams.sh" and the folder mutabledreams into roms/ports on the SD card (the
  SHARE partition). Update the game list in Knulli's menu, then start Mutable Dreams under Ports.

THE BUTTONS
  The H700 handhelds have no F1, F2 or FN: tap SELECT for the menu (every place, the options, and QUIT at its end:
  press UP once there, then A); hold SELECT for the help. Tap L2 for the quick bar (PLAY MODE, LIGHT PICTURE,
  DEUTSCH / ENGLISH at its end). Every version starts in English.

THE SCREEN
  The launcher reads the screen's size and draws the 4:3 stage to fit: 640 x 480 fills it; on 720 x 720 (CubeXX) and
  720 x 480 (RG34XX) the stage sits in the middle. log.txt says what was found ("screen: ...").
  The picture goes straight into the window first, as on the Brick. If no picture comes within 20 s, the launcher
  starts it once more through SDL's renderer and keeps the way that worked (mutabledreams/video-mode.txt).

SWITCH FILES (in mutabledreams/, content does not matter)
  light.on      the light picture from the start (it also comes by itself when the stage is slow)
  calm.on       CALM MOTION from the start
  edge.off      no glow of the garden's colours in the black side bars (screens wider than 4:3)
  nosplash.on   no animated start screen
  selftest.on   plays 30 s by itself and writes its measurements (frame rate, sound) to log.txt
  keytest.on    every button pressed is written to log.txt
  surface.on / nosurface.on   only the window's own pixels / only SDL's renderer
  gpu.on        SDL's accelerated (GL) renderer
  nofast.on     the CPU governor left alone (else "performance" while it runs, the old one put back after; no overclock)
  usbaudio.off  the sound stays on the handheld even with a USB-C sound card plugged in
