MUTABLE DREAMS FOR TRIMUI BRICK - release 1.6.2 (candidate) - by Kid Bunka

Install (Knulli): copy "Mutable Dreams.sh" and the folder "mutabledreams" into roms/ports on the SD card,
then update the game list. Start "Mutable Dreams" from Ports. Quit: MENU > QUIT.

Controls
  D-pad up/down   choose K1..K4          R2 + up/down      model of the track
  D-pad left/right turn it (L2: fine)    R2 + left/right   Biome / tone preset
  L1 / R1         track                  R2 + L1 / R1      octave
  A B X Y         SEL BACK EDIT FX       R2 + A B X Y      LOCK FREEZE ENV GLOBAL
  START / SELECT  PLAY / WHERE AM I      R2 + START        REC     R2 + SELECT (hold 1 s) SAVE
  MENU            jump menu              F2                MIXER view       R2 + F2   BIOME ATLAS
  F1              SOUND LAB              R2 + F1           REC
  PLAY MODE       L2 (the quick bar) -> PLAY MODE: D-pad + ABXY play eight scale tones, R2 adds a chord (with
                  POLY on: on the polyphonic voice, else on the selected track). SELECT ends it at once.
                  The Brick's side FN switch does not reach the program and is not used.
  L2 (tap)        the quick bar: every place (garden, mixer, Sound Lab, atlas, sequence, dream map, worlds,
                  archive, modulation, voice, space, playground, help), MENU and PLAY MODE - on handhelds without MENU, F1,
                  F2 or the FN switch (RGB20S, Miyoo) this is how to reach them; see RGB20S.txt or MIYOO.txt
  B is always one step back, the way you came (Sound Lab, a view, an FM-1 page); at home it stays home.
  B held: home at once, from anywhere.
  SELECT tapped only says where you are; held: the help. Top right, beside the place: B and where B leads.
  L2 tapped opens the quick bar on the place you last left: L2, A goes back and forth between two places.
  Hold any FM-1 button for its help.

THE GARDEN FOLLOWS THE MUSIC: in a Biome whose sky has an aurora, and on the journey Aurora Polaris, six
  ribbons (one per track, in its colour) flare with each of its notes and float higher for higher notes. On the
  journey Terra Pluvia it rains, and each note splashes at the foot of its plant. Always with the note itself.
THE GUIDE: twelve pictured pages on how it all works, for anyone. It opens by itself on the very first start;
  later: hold SELECT (the button map), then X. LEFT/RIGHT or A turn the pages, Y the button map, B closes.
Start screen: a night garden wakes (aurora, six plants that grow and bloom, the title rising letter by letter);
  any button fades into the stage (and does nothing else); after 10 s it goes by itself. An empty file
  mutabledreams/nosplash.on starts without it.
The GARDEN (home, on the LIVE page): a living night garden instead of the FM-1's screen. Each track is a plant
  whose species follows its sound (tulip, fern, bells, dandelion, reeds, mushrooms); it grows with its level,
  blooms on each hit and sends a spark up with the note; a muted track sleeps. K1..K4 are the weather:
  FIREFLIES (density), WIND (motion), GLOW (colour), MIST (space, also the pond's mirror). The moon crosses the
  sky with the bar, a butterfly marks the chosen track. MENU > GARDEN / FM-1 SCREEN switches back to the FM-1's
  screen; every other page, help and questions show it anyway.
  The full views and the Sound Lab share the garden's look: its night sky behind glass panels, the mixer's faders
  as stems with a flower that opens with the level, the sequence's hits as seedlings (the playing one blooms),
  the dream map's stations as flowers.
  Around it too: the stage's night with hills, the key hints as pebbles, the transport as a flower (a bud when
  stopped), FREEZE and LOCK with a snowflake and a padlock, and MENU in two columns (the Brick's own views and
  the FM-1 pages; LEFT/RIGHT changes side).
  The FM-1's own pages glow as light over the garden's night, with smoothed edges (Scale3x); with
  MENU > HOME: FM-1 SCREEN they keep the FM-1's original pixels. A Biome change lets the new garden sprout.
  The garden answers the music: each hit ripples the pond, the fireflies light up on the beat; FREEZE ices
  the pond over, frosts the plants and lets it snow; the Biome drift turns night into dawn towards scene B
  (the moon becomes the sun).
  In play mode eight stepping stones lie over the pond, one per scale tone, and light up and ripple when
  played; the butterfly flies over to the track you choose.
  More creatures: living links are glowing vines between the plants (a light runs along them), POLY's voices
  float as lanterns on the pond, a frog jumps whenever RANDOM plays, SHIMMER is a band of light in the sky,
  LOCK a lantern on the bank, and a muted track falls asleep slowly (and wakes again).
PLAYGROUND (MENU > PLAYGROUND, or the quick bar): ten toys (L1/R1 turn the carousel) that play the chosen track in the Biome's scale.
  BOUNCE: balls bounce in a box, each bounce rings the lily pad it lands on (eight scale tones; the ceiling an
  octave up, the walls root and fifth). A throws a ball, LEFT/RIGHT tilt, UP/DOWN gravity (none: they float),
  Y empties. ORBITS: up to five planets circle a sun and sing when they pass the gate at the top, each at its
  own speed (1/4 .. 4 turns a bar): polyrhythms. UP/DOWN planet, LEFT/RIGHT its speed, A on/off, Y its note.
  RAIN: a cloud rains on the lily pads. LEFT/RIGHT move it, UP/DOWN more or less rain, A thunder, Y IN TIME
  (drops on the sixteenths). SWING: a pendulum wave - eight pendulums drift apart into waves and come together
  again at the end of the cycle; each rings its tone in the middle. A releases them again, LEFT/RIGHT the cycle
  (20 .. 90 s), UP/DOWN faster/slower, Y one way. FIREFLY: steer a firefly (D-pad) to the glowing notes; each one
  caught joins a melody that loops in time (up to 16), and the trail behind it is that melody. A forgets it,
  Y quarters / eighths / sixteenths. WHEEL: four rhythm circles, each its hits spread evenly round its steps
  (3 in 8, 5 in 16 ...), each ring a track: a drum circle. UP/DOWN ring, LEFT/RIGHT hits, Y steps, A turn it.
  ECHO, a game: the pond sings a phrase on four lily pads, you sing it back with the D-pad; each round one note
  longer and a little faster. A starts; the best round is kept.
  SEED, a game that plays the sound itself: fly a dandelion seed on the night wind with the D-pad. Its height is
  the garden's GLOW, how far right it flies is the WIND - turned live as you fly. Spores of light ring a tone
  and thicken the FIREFLIES; burrs thicken the MIST and break the streak. A: a gust (a chord). Y: the four dials
  back as they were when you came.
  JELLY, the other two dials: a jellyfish in a deep pond. A pulses its bell (up, a soft low note), it sinks
  slowly; LEFT/RIGHT drift. Its depth is the MIST, how far right it swims the FIREFLIES; glowing plankton rings
  when the bell touches it. Y: the dials back.
  STARFALL, a catching game: stars fall in the tempo, each in one of eight lanes (the scale's tones, low left to
  high right), in little melodies. LEFT/RIGHT move a lotus cup under them; every star caught rings its tone.
  A begins a round with three moons (three stars missed and it ends; it quickens as you catch), Y gentle /
  steady / quick. The best round is kept with the tale.
  All: L1/R1 the toy, X the track that plays, B back.
DREAM TALES: a story in eight chapters, told while you play. Each chapter has a few lines and a small deed (the
  playground shows it at the top); when it is done, its card rises over the stage and the next chapter begins.
  How far the tale has come is kept in mutabledreams/save/tale.txt (delete it to begin again).
  KEEP: R2 + X turns what the chosen track played in the last bar into its sequence (16 steps, each note on its
  nearest sixteenth): a toy's music stays in the song. R2 + A brings back the sequence as it was.
WORLDS (MENU > WORLDS): 128 whole scenes in 20 families (Gardens of Number, Bells and Resonance, Drones and
  Overtones, Rain and Water, Night Choirs, Endless, Voices and Poems, Machines and Automata, Plaits Orchestra,
  Rhythm Gardens, Resonant Rooms, Sky Machines, Forest Floor, Deep Waters, Glass and Air,
  Lullabies, Seasons, Desert and Dunes, City After Dark, Moon and Tides), each grown from one of
  the engine's Biomes with its own tempo, macros, Sound Lab, SYNTH and LOGIC, and a line of story. A enters one,
  Y wanders to the family's next world every few minutes (L/R: how many). mutabledreams/worlds/worlds.txt is
  plain text: change the worlds or add your own.
AMBIENCE BEDS (Sound Lab > TEXTURES: FOREST, AIR = the wind, WATER = the rain, STARDUST) start silent at every
  start; turn them up by hand, or enter a world that brings them (Forest Floor, Deep Waters and a few more).
BUTTONS (MENU > BUTTONS): a drawn Brick with every button labelled, page by page (garden, with R2, Sound Lab,
  play mode, full views; L1/R1 turn the pages). Shown once after the very first start.
Full views (F2, R2 + F2, MENU): MIXER (faders, pan, sends, mutes, meters), BIOME ATLAS (every Biome by family;
  LEFT from the left column goes to the families, UP/DN there picks one, RIGHT goes back to its Biomes; A loads it;
  with unsaved changes or while playing, A again changes now; Y loads a surprise Biome; its whole name stands below), SEQUENCE (all six tracks' steps; X edits a track as a piano roll:
  L/R step, UP/DN value, L1/R1 note / velocity / chance / gate / rule / length, A step on/off, Y generate, X done;
  with R2: L1 copy, R1 paste, UP/DN transpose, L/R octave, A mirror, Y step or phrase, X queue a fill),
  DREAM MAP (R2 + MENU: an Itinera journey's route of Biomes, chapter by chapter; choose journey, length, pace and
  room, A dreams, A again wakes; Y: FREE FLIGHT - the wisp is yours, the D-pad flies it, A over an island lands there
  and loads its Biome, a falling star caught nudges a macro), MODULATION (MENU: each track's LFO A and B as curves, edit target, shape, depth
  and rate; X edits the living links: source, signal, destination, target, depth, release, A on/off; X again
  edits the four macros (UP/DN macro, L/R value: density, motion, colour, space; a dot shows where the drift
  takes each); X again edits the Biome drift: L1 / R1 capture scene A / B (drift off), UP/DN field, L/R value: mode off / manual /
  auto, position between A and B, cycle 10-300 s, depth; Y: the FIREFLY POND - each track a lily pad, its two LFOs
  as fireflies: L1/R1 next firefly, UP/DN higher (depth), L/R slower/faster, A its dance (shape), X what it
  touches (target), Y back),
  VOICE (MENU: one track's whole sound in five panels with pictures: SOUND preset / model / tone / harmonics,
  FILTER cutoff / resonance / character / breathe, ENVELOPE attack / decay / shape / level, RHYTHM hits / length /
  rotation / chance, PLAY note / accent / repeats / clock div; L1/R1 track, UP/DN field, L/R value, X next panel,
  hold A to listen),
  SPACE (MENU: the shared effects with live pictures: ECHO time / feedback, room tail, return; GRAIN memory
  space / size / position / feedback with the grains playing; TAPE pitch / direction / freeze / noise as reels;
  DUCK from a track / amount / release and SWING; UP/DN field, L/R value, X or L1/R1 panel, A freeze).

Sound Lab (F1), its modules in two rows - SOURCES and EFFECTS: textures, SAMPLES (your WAV files from mutabledreams/samples: loops or hits that follow a
  track), POLY (eight voices with all 24 synthesis models: play mode, RANDOM or a track play it),
  granular cloud, PARTICLES (granular / stretch / looping delay / spectral), SEEDS
  (grains and delay on tape or digital memory), shimmer reverb,
  CROSSMOD (one track cross-modulates another, vocoder), RANDOM (deja vu melodies in the Biome's key on a track),
  per-track sends, ensemble and tape, master and limiter.
  VOICES: spoken and sung voices (Ogg or WAV in mutabledreams/samples/voices/, e.g. public domain recordings
  from LibriVox), cut at the breaths: SENTENCE, FRAGMENTS, VOWEL (a held vowel), MANTRA (one sentence in the
  bars), GRAINS (a granular sampler on the voice itself: position, scan, grain size, grains per second, spray,
  scatter in the scale); FREE, on a track's hits, every bar or MARBLES (a Marbles of its own decides whether and
  where, deja vu brings words back); TIDE: Mutable Instruments Tides 2 moving position, pitch, size, grains,
  spray or level; pitch, reverse chance, RADIO filter, sends. In the garden a bird sings along and the title
  floats by.
  SYNTH: six instruments of the garden: JUST DRONE (additive, just intonation: harmonic series, 4:5:6,
  septimal), MODAL GARDEN (resonators struck by rain), SHEPARD (rising or falling without end), KARPLUS RAIN
  (plucked strings), GOLDEN BELLS (FM at an irrational ratio: sqrt 2, golden ratio, pi/2, e/2), FORMANT CHOIR
  (a vowel choir, the chord moving I IV V vi), RINGS (Mutable Instruments Rings: modal, strings, FM, chords)
  and ELEMENTS (Mutable Instruments Elements: bowed, blown, struck, CHARACTER goes from bow to breath to mallet);
  played by ITSELF, by LOGIC (VOICE TO: SYNTH) or a track's hits.
  LOGIC: music from musical mathematics, two voices in the Biome's key onto POLY, SYNTH or the tracks: TINTINNABULI
  (Part), PHASING (Reich), FIBONACCI (golden word and angle, a canon), AUTOMATON (cellular rules 30/90/110...),
  MARKOV (temperature), L-SYSTEM (growing grammars), HARMONIC (the root's overtones, just intonation on POLY);
  SHAPE, DENSITY, RANGE, SEED, VARIATION, RATE, GATE. Each rule drawn on its page.
  GRIDS: Mutable Instruments' topographic drum map: MAP X / MAP Y choose a place among 25 patterns, KICK, SNARE
  and HAT DENSITY, CHAOS, ACCENT, RATE; each part plays a track, the kit, POLY or SYNTH while the transport runs.
  RACK A and RACK B (EFFECTS): VOICES, SAMPLES, SYNTH and POLY can each go INTO a rack before they meet the mix:
  FILTER (low / band / high pass, RESONANCE, FOLLOW the sound's loudness, SWEEP), DRIVE, CRUSH, RESONATOR
  (Mutable Instruments Rings tuned to the Biome's key: modal, sympathetic strings, string, FM, chords, string +
  reverb; STRUCTURE, BRIGHTNESS, DAMPING, POSITION) and a tape ECHO in time (1/16 .. 1/2, FEEDBACK, WOW, TONE,
  crossing sides). Every stage at 0 costs nothing. The page draws the chain with the sound flowing through it.
  ARCHIVE (MENU): free voices to fetch over WiFi - German and English poems from LibriVox (archive.org),
  authors dead more than 70 years, public domain / CC0 / CC-BY. A fetches one (with the system's curl) into
  mutabledreams/samples/archive/ with its credit beside it; A again puts it into VOICES. Only what
  mutabledreams/archive/catalogue.tsv lists can be fetched. X opens the SAMPLER: VOICE, GRAINS, TIDE and
  MODULES (into Cloud, Clouds, Beads, Shimmer, Rings, echo, filter sweep; a module opens when turned up).
  L1 / R1 module · up/down parameter · left/right value (L2 fine) · A neutral and back · B back, F1 close, L2 held with up/down: five rows at a time
  Everything at neutral leaves the sound exactly as Mutable Dreams 2.1. Settings: mutabledreams/save/lab.bin
  Per Biome: MASTER > THIS BIOME > OWN SOUND LAB keeps the Lab with this Biome; it comes back whenever the Biome
  does (by hand, from a bank, on an Itinera journey), gliding over in LAB CHANGE TIME (3 s; 0 = at once).
  MASTER itself is the same for all Biomes.

MASTER (the last module of the Sound Lab), the mastering chain in the order of the signal: SOUND sets it all at
  once (CLEAN, GENTLE, WARM, LOUD, AIRY; turning any row makes it OWN), LOW CUT, a three-band EQ (low shelf, bell,
  high shelf, each frequency to choose), TILT, SATURATION (tape, tube or transistor, anti-aliased, level matched),
  a GLUE COMPRESSOR (attack, release, parallel mix, automatic make-up), WIDTH, GAIN and a look-ahead LIMITER with its
  CEILING. The Lab shows the EQ's curve and what the compressor and the limiter take away. Always at the very end:
  a soft guard, so the sound never clips hard.
While it runs, the launcher sets the CPU governor to "performance" (the cores at Knulli's normal top speed, no
  overclock) and gives the old one back when it ends; an empty file mutabledreams/nofast.on leaves it alone.
  The sound goes to the Brick's 48 kHz codec through Mutable Dreams' own resampler (polyphase sinc), in float,
  and only then to 16 bits. mutabledreams/profile.on: log.txt says every 30 s where the picture's time goes.
CALM MOTION (the quick bar's last entry): what only moves for decoration holds still (breathing shapes, swaying
  vines, drifting spores and pollen); what shows the music still moves. mutabledreams/calm.on starts with it.
  Under the tempo the transport is written out: PLAYING or RESTING.
LIGHT PICTURE (the quick bar's second-to-last entry): less decoration (no spores, no mirror in the pond, no grain), a
  faster stage. It comes on by itself when the stage stays slow; mutabledreams/light.on starts with it.

  and the Mini Flip ask for 20 frames a second (they drew one in 42 ms with 1.5.7 / 1.5.8); fps15.on: 15.

  detuned saws through a breathing filter), CZ CRYSTAL (phase distortion, plucked crystal to soft brass) and WIND FLUTE
  (breath in a resonant tube), each drawn in the Sound Lab; six worlds for them (Glass and Air). In the Sound Lab a
  card says for four seconds what the chosen row does. The left column's card grounds are kept instead of blended
  anew each frame (about a third fewer see-through pixels). Lab module names are the same in both languages.
OPTIONS (1.1, the quick bar's OPTIONS stone, or Y in the button map): swap A and B, swap X and Y, give any of A B X Y
  L1 R1 L2 R2 START SELECT another's part (the next button pressed takes it), how held buttons repeat (calm, normal,
  quick), language, light picture, calm motion, the button map, the guide. The hints, the button map and the guide
  name the buttons as you have them. START + SELECT held 3 s, anywhere: every button itself again.
SMOOTHER (1.1): the stage is drawn on a core of its own while the last frame is shown; buttons come through at
  once; held arrows keep going (and quicken) in the menu, the quick bar, the guide and the options.
DEUTSCH / ENGLISH (the quick bar's last stone): the whole interface in German or English, kept between starts
  (1.5.9: every version starts in English until German is chosen here). The table is plain text: mutabledreams/lang/de.txt (GLOSSAR.md
  beside it). Names of sound parameters, modules, Biomes and worlds stay English, as on the FM-1.
USB-C AUDIO: a class-compliant USB sound card or USB-C headphone adapter plugged into the Brick takes the sound
  (USB AUDIO is said, with its name); pulled out, the sound goes back to the Brick's speaker. log.txt lists every
  output the system sees, USB ones marked. An empty file mutabledreams/usbaudio.off keeps the sound on the Brick.
KEYBOARD: mutabledreams/keys.txt (written on the first start) binds keyboard keys to the Brick's buttons,
  "A = X, Space"; the hints then name your keys. Delete it for the defaults.
MANUAL: Mutable-Dreams-Handbuch-DE.pdf and Mutable-Dreams-Manual-EN.pdf, pictured, beside this package.
The picture goes straight into the window's surface (measured faster on the Brick: 11.8 instead of 16.5 ms a
  frame); an empty file mutabledreams/nosurface.on goes back to SDL's renderer.
MIDI: a USB keyboard or controller (class compliant, through the Brick's USB port under Knulli). Its notes
  play POLY when POLY is up (POLY > MIDI NOTES: all channels or one; velocity, sustain pedal, pitch bend, mod wheel
  onto MORPH); otherwise, and for everything else (controllers, MIDI learn, clock), Mutable Dreams answers as the
  FM-1 does over USB: channels 1-6 play the tracks. MIDI goes in only: Mutable Dreams sends no notes or clock out
  (so no per-track recording into a computer yet). Unplugging a keyboard lets go of every note it held.

Saved Biomes and settings: mutabledreams/save/ (brick-prefs.txt keeps CALM MOTION, LIGHT PICTURE and the
  playground's last toy between starts; tale.txt how far the dream tale has come). Log: mutabledreams/log.txt.
