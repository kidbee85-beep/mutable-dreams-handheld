VOICES - spoken and sung voices for the Sound Lab (Mutable Dreams for Trimui Brick, by Kid Bunka)

Put Ogg Vorbis (.ogg) or WAV files of voices here: poems, stories, speeches, chants, mantras. The Sound Lab's
VOICES module cuts them at the breaths into sentences and word fragments and plays them into the music
(SENTENCE, FRAGMENTS, VOWEL, MANTRA). At most the first 6 minutes of a file are used.

A text file with the same name beside a sound (e.g. poem.ogg + poem.txt) gives it a title (line 1) and a credit
(line 2: author, reader, licence). They show in the Lab and in the garden.

Free voices: LibriVox (librivox.org) records public domain texts and releases the recordings into the public
domain; the files are on archive.org as .ogg. In Germany and the EU a text is free when its author died more
than 70 years ago. Use only what you may use: public domain, CC0, or CC-BY with the credit kept.
