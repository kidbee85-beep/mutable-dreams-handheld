Put your own WAV files here: field recordings, textures, single hits.
16, 24 or 32-bit PCM or 32-bit float, mono or stereo, any sample rate, up to 90 s each, up to 64 files.
In Mutable Dreams: F1 (Sound Lab) > SAMPLES > FILE. Press A on FILE after adding files.
LOOP plays a window of the file as a bed; FOLLOW TRACK plays it on every note of a track (pitched by the note).
