Put your own WAV files here: field recordings, textures, single hits.
16, 24 or 32-bit PCM or 32-bit float, mono or stereo, any sample rate, up to 90 s each, up to 64 files.
In Mutable Dreams: F1 (Sound Lab) > SAMPLES > FILE. Press A on FILE after adding files.
LOOP plays a window of the file as a bed; FOLLOW TRACK plays it on every note of a track (pitched by the note).

Ten sounds come with the game (since 1.6): kalimba-c4, glass-bell-c4, soft-pad-c3, sub-drone-c2 (pitched: C4 / C3 / C2 as
recorded), rain-loop, wind-loop, water-drops, vinyl-crackle-loop (beds for LOOP), soft-kick and shaker (single hits for
FOLLOW TRACK). They are made by arithmetic alone (tools/make_samples.py), with no recording and no third-party material,
and are given away under CC0 (public domain). Delete or replace them as you like; they are not put back over your own.
