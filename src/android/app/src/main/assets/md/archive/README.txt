ARCHIVE - Mutable Dreams for Trimui Brick, by Kid Bunka

catalogue.tsv lists the voices the ARCHIVE view (MENU > ARCHIVE) can fetch: poems read for LibriVox, stored at
archive.org. Each was checked when the list was made: a free licence (public domain, CC0, public domain mark or
CC-BY), an author who died more than 70 years ago, 20 s to 4 minutes. A fetched poem lands in
../samples/archive/<item>/ with a .txt beside it: title, author with years, reader, source and licence. Keep the
.txt when you use a voice in a recording or on the radio.

Columns: basket, archive.org item, file, title, author, born, died, reader, seconds, bytes, licence.
Made with tools/gen_archive_catalogue.py (in the port's sources).
