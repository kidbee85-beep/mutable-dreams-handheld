Mutable Dreams

A garden of sound that makes music by itself and answers you.
Version {{VERSION}} · by Kid Bunka · English

This manual walks you through everything Mutable Dreams can do. Read it from the start, or open just the chapter you need. Every picture comes from the program itself and shows exactly what you see on the Brick.

Welcome to the dream garden

Mutable Dreams is a generative instrument. It plays by itself, the way a garden grows by itself. You do not need to read music or program anything. You tend the garden, and it answers you. Turn a dial, choose another Biome or play a note in play mode, and the music changes in a way you can hear and see.

At its heart runs the sound engine of Mutable Dreams 2.1 (FM-1) with six tracks. Around it are:

  • the Sound Lab, with modules after Mutable Instruments (Plaits, Rings, Elements, Clouds, Beads, Marbles, Grids, Tides);
  • the Playground, with ten toys and little games;
  • Worlds, ready-made scenes of sound;
  • a dream tale that leads you through all of it while you play.
The start screen: a night garden wakes. Any button starts the program, or it continues by itself after ten seconds.

What you will find

  • The garden: six plants are the six voices of the music, and four dials are its weather.
  • Biomes: 272 sound worlds of the engine, in families, from bells to storms.
  • Worlds: 96 complete scenes, each with its own tempo, Sound Lab settings and a short story.
  • Playground: bouncing balls, planets, rain, pendulums and more toys that make music with you.
  • Sound Lab: synthesizers, grain clouds, reverb, echo and resonators, with mastering at the end.
  • English and German: the whole interface can be switched between them.

Installing and the first start

On the Trimui Brick (Knulli)

  1. Unpack Mutable-Dreams-Brick-{{VERSION}}.zip.
  2. Copy the file Mutable Dreams.sh and the folder mutabledreams into roms/ports on the SD card.
  3. Update the game list in the Knulli menu, then start Mutable Dreams from Ports.

You can copy a new version over an older one. Your saved Biomes, settings, tale progress and samples stay as they are. They live in mutabledreams/save and mutabledreams/samples.

To quit, press MENU, go to the bottom of the right-hand column, choose Quit and press A.

On the Miyoo Mini, Mini Plus and Mini Flip (Onion OS)

Unpack Mutable-Dreams-Miyoo-Mini-Plus-Onion-{{VERSION}}.zip (Mini Plus) or Mutable-Dreams-Miyoo-Mini-Onion-{{VERSION}}.zip (Mini and Mini Flip) and copy its folders App and Roms onto the SD card, merging them with the ones there. Start Mutable Dreams under Ports or Apps. These handhelds show a lighter picture at up to 20 frames a second; MIYOO.txt beside the program lists switch files for other settings.

On the RGB20S (AmberELEC)

Unpack Mutable-Dreams-RGB20S-AmberELEC-{{VERSION}}.zip into roms/ports, update the game list and start Mutable Dreams under Ports.

On the Anbernic H700 handhelds (Knulli)

For the RG35XX, RG40XX, RG CubeXX and RG34XX with Knulli: unpack Mutable-Dreams-Anbernic-H700-Knulli-{{VERSION}}.zip and copy Mutable Dreams.sh and mutabledreams into roms/ports. The launcher reads the screen's size and fits the stage to it; H700.txt lists its switch files. This package is a community test: it has not run on an H700 yet, so please send log.txt if something is wrong.

On Android

Install Mutable-Dreams-Android-{{VERSION}}.apk. Its files (saves, your samples, worlds) live in Android/data/com.kidbunka.mutabledreams/files. Without a gamepad you play by touch (see The buttons).

On every handheld without a MENU key, tap SELECT for the menu. To quit, press UP once there (that is Quit, at the end of the list) and press A.

On a PC: Dream Garden for Mutable Dreams Linux

The package Mutable-Dreams-Linux-OS-x86_64 installs the program as Dream Garden, with an icon in the start menu. Without a game controller, every hint names the keyboard keys instead (see The buttons).

The garden

The garden is your home. On the left stand the six tracks, each with its number, its sound model and a plant. In the middle the garden itself grows.

  • Each track is a plant whose kind follows its sound: tulip, fern, bells, dandelion, reeds or mushrooms.
  • A plant grows with its track's level, blooms on each note and sends a spark upwards.
  • A muted track slowly falls asleep, and wakes again when you unmute it.
The garden while the music plays: the six tracks on the left; the four dials, tempo and key on the right.

The four dials: the garden's weather

The four dials stand on the right. UP and DOWN choose one, LEFT and RIGHT turn it.

DialWhat it doesIn the picture
FIREFLIESDensity: how many notes are playedfireflies in the air
WINDMotion: how much the music changesthe plants sway
GLOWColour: dark or bright, soft or radiantthe light in the sky
MISTSpace: reverb and widthmist, and the mirror in the pond
Start with MIST: a little more space makes almost any Biome more dreamlike. If the music feels too even, turn up WIND.

Playing, tempo and Biomes

START starts the music, and START again stops it. Under the tempo it says PLAYING or RESTING, and a ring of dots shows the steps of the bar.

Each sound world of the engine is a Biome, and there are 272 of them. R2 with LEFT/RIGHT moves to the next Biome. The Biome atlas (R2 + F2) shows them all. When the Biome changes, new plants sprout in the garden.

While you hold R2, two rows show everything it does together with the other buttons.

The garden follows the music

  • Each note ripples the pond, and the fireflies light up on the beat.
  • FREEZE (R2 + B) ices the pond over and lets it snow.
  • In Biomes with an aurora, six ribbons flare with each note, rising higher for higher notes.
  • On the journey Terra Pluvia it rains, and each note splashes at the foot of its plant.
  • A frog jumps whenever RANDOM plays, and POLY's voices float as lanterns on the pond.
  • The Biome drift slowly turns night into dawn, and the moon becomes the sun.

Quick bar and menu

The quick bar (tap L2)

A short tap on L2 opens the quick bar. Every place lies there as a living stone on a vine.

  • LEFT/RIGHT choose a stone, A goes there, and B closes the bar.
  • UP/DOWN jump between the places and the settings (options, play mode, picture, language) at the bar's end.
  • A leaf above the chosen stone gives its name and a line about what you will find there.
The quick bar, with the leaf above the chosen stone.

The quick bar always opens on the place you left last. So L2, A takes you straight back and forth between two places. At its end lie four switches:

StoneWhat it switches
PLAY MODEThe buttons play notes (for handhelds without the FN switch).
LIGHT PICTURELess decoration and a faster stage. It also switches on by itself when the picture is slow.
CALM MOTIONWhat only moves for decoration holds still. What shows the music keeps moving.
DEUTSCH / ENGLISHThe language of the whole interface. Your choice is saved.

The menu (MENU)

The menu shows the Brick's places as stones along a garden path on the left, and the FM-1's pages as leaves on the right.

  • UP/DOWN walk the path, LEFT/RIGHT cross to the other side, and A goes there.
  • The path glows up to the chosen stone, and a butterfly follows along.
  • Quit is the last leaf on the right: from the top, one press of UP reaches it.
  • On handhelds without a MENU key, SELECT opens and closes the menu.
The menu: the Brick's places on the left, the FM-1's pages on the right.

Playing yourself: play mode

Slide the FN switch on the side, and the buttons become notes.

  • The D-pad and A B X Y play eight tones of the Biome's scale, so nothing can sound wrong.
  • R2 adds a chord, and L1/R1 choose the track.
  • Eight stepping stones lie over the pond, one per tone, and light up as you play.
Play mode: the right side shows which button plays which tone; the stepping stones lie over the pond. If the POLY voice is on in the Sound Lab, you play it: eight voices with all 24 synthesis models of Plaits. Otherwise you play the chosen track.

On handhelds without the FN switch, switch play mode on and off from the quick bar.

The Sound Lab

F1 opens the Sound Lab. Here the sound itself can be bent, folded and set alight. Its modules stand in two rows: sources and effects. Each module draws a live picture of what it is doing.

  • L1/R1 choose a module, and UP/DOWN choose a setting.
  • LEFT/RIGHT change it; hold L2 for fine steps.
  • A sets the setting back to neutral.
  • X silences the whole module, and X again brings it back: the quickest way to hear what an effect adds.
  • Y jumps to the next module that is sounding, so you never have to page through silent ones.
  • L1 and R1 pressed together (or F2) show all modules at once: the sources above, the effects below. The D-pad chooses, A goes there. This is the quickest way into the effects.
The Sound Lab: the modules at the top, the module's picture in the middle, its settings below. With everything at neutral, the sound is exactly that of Mutable Dreams 2.1. Nothing can break: A brings any setting back.

One Sound Lab is shared by all Biomes. You can also give a Biome its own Lab under MASTER › THIS BIOME › OWN SOUND LAB. It then returns with that Biome every time, gliding over in three seconds.

The garden shows the SYNTH too: glass bubbles for the glass harmonica, crystals for CZ, breath for the flute, bands of light for the pad, and sparks for the others. Not sure what a row does? Choose it: for four seconds a card at the bottom of the picture says, in one line, what it changes.

The full views

Beside the garden there are views that each show one thing in full. Reach them from the menu or the quick bar; each one shows its buttons at the bottom.

Mixer (F2)

The level, pan and sends of the six tracks. Each fader is a stem, with a flower that opens with the level.

  • LEFT/RIGHT choose the track, and UP/DOWN change the value.
  • L1/R1 change the row, and A mutes the track.
The mixer.

Biome atlas (R2 + F2)

All 272 Biomes, by family. Choose the family on the left and the Biome on the right; A loads it. If music is playing or there are unsaved changes, a second A changes at once. Y loads a surprise Biome.

The Biome atlas.

Sequence

The steps of all six tracks at a glance. Hits are seedlings, and the one playing blooms. X edits a track as a piano roll:

  • LEFT/RIGHT choose the step, and UP/DOWN change its value.
  • L1/R1 choose the field: note, velocity, chance, gate, rule or track length.
  • A turns the step on or off, and Y generates a new pattern.

With R2 held you get tools: copy, paste, transpose, octave, mirror, and a fill for the next bar.

The sequence of all tracks. Editing one track.

Dream map (R2 + MENU)

Travel through Biomes for hours, chapter by chapter. Choose the journey, its length, pace and room. A lets you dream, and A again wakes you.

Y starts free flight. You steer a wisp over the islands with the D-pad, and A lands on an island and loads its Biome. Catching a falling star nudges a dial.

The dream map with its stations. Free flight over the islands.

Modulation

Each track has two LFOs, shown as curves: their target, shape, depth and rate.

  • X goes on to the living links, which connect tracks. For example, one track's level can make another's filter breathe.
  • X again goes to the four macros and to the Biome drift between two scenes, A and B.
  • Y opens the firefly pond. Each track is a lily pad, and its two LFOs are fireflies: how high one flies is its depth, and how fast it dances is its rate.
The LFOs of the six tracks. The firefly pond.

Voice

One track's whole sound in five pictured panels: sound, filter, envelope, rhythm and play. X moves to the next panel. Hold A to hear the track alone.

One track's voice.

Space

The shared effects, each with a live picture:

  • Echo and room: the echoes and the reverb tail.
  • Grain memory: the memory drawn as a tape, with the grains that are playing.
  • Tape: two reels whose speed is the pitch.
  • Ducking and swing.

A freezes it all, and the picture turns ice blue.

Space.

Archive

Free voices to fetch over WiFi: German and English poems from LibriVox, in the public domain or freely licensed.

  • A fetches a poem, and A again puts it into VOICES.
  • X opens the sampler, which sends the voice into grains, Tides or the Lab's modules.
The archive, with poems to fetch.

Worlds

A world is a whole scene: a Biome with its own tempo, dials, Sound Lab, SYNTH, LOGIC and a line of story. There are 96 worlds in 16 families:

Gardens of Number · Bells and Resonance · Drones and Overtones · Rain and Water · Night Choirs · Endless · Voices and Poems · Machines and Automata · Plaits Orchestra · Rhythm Gardens · Resonant Rooms · Sky Machines · Forest Floor · Deep Waters · Glass and Air · Lullabies

  • L1/R1 choose the family, UP/DOWN the world, and A enters it.
  • Y wanders on to the family's next world every few minutes, and LEFT/RIGHT set how many minutes.
Worlds: the families on the left, their worlds on the right, and the chosen world's story and contents below. The nature sounds (forest, wind and rain) are only on in the worlds they belong to, such as Forest Floor and Deep Waters. That way the garden starts out clear. The Lullabies set the sleep timer themselves when you enter one, so the music fades out while you fall asleep.

Your own worlds

The worlds are plain text in mutabledreams/worlds/worlds.txt. Change them or add your own. A world looks like this:

[MY FAMILY] Moon Garden biome = 89 story = A song walks out and comes home again. tempo = 60 macros = 30 25 40 70 lab = SHIMMER.MIX 30; TEXTURES.FOREST 20

The playground

On the playground, ten toys make music with you. They always play the chosen track in the Biome's scale, so they always fit. For every toy:

  • L1/R1 turn the carousel to the next toy.
  • X chooses the track that plays, and B goes back.
  • R2 + X keeps what that track played in the last bar as its sequence, so a toy's music stays in the song. R2 + A undoes this.
Bounce: Balls bounce in a box, and every bounce rings. On the floor it is the lily pad the ball lands on; on the ceiling, the same tone an octave up. A throws a ball, LEFT/RIGHT tilt the box, and UP/DOWN set the gravity. Orbits: Up to five planets circle a sun. Each sings when it passes the gate at the top, at its own speed, so polyrhythms appear. A turns a planet on or off, and Y gives it a new note. Rain: A cloud rains on the lily pads. LEFT/RIGHT move it, UP/DOWN set how much it rains, A brings thunder, and Y makes the drops fall in time. Swing: Eight pendulums drift apart into waves and come together again at the end of the cycle. A releases them again, and LEFT/RIGHT set the cycle. Firefly: Steer a firefly to the glowing notes. Each one caught joins a melody that loops in time. A forgets the melody, and Y chooses quarters, eighths or sixteenths. Wheel: Four rhythm circles, each one a track, like a drum circle. LEFT/RIGHT set the hits, Y the steps, and A turns the ring. Echo (a game): The pond sings a phrase, and you sing it back with the D-pad. Each round adds one note. Your best round is kept. Seed (a game): Fly a dandelion seed on the night wind. Its height is GLOW, and how far right it flies is WIND. Spores of light ring and thicken the FIREFLIES; burrs thicken the MIST. Jelly: A pulses a jellyfish upwards, and it slowly sinks again. Its depth is the MIST, and how far right it swims is the FIREFLIES. Plankton rings when it touches it. Starfall (a game): Stars fall in time in eight lanes, one per tone. LEFT/RIGHT move a lotus cup under them, and every star you catch rings. A starts a round with three moons, and Y sets the speed.
Play with the orbits until you like a rhythm, then press R2 + X. The rhythm stays as the track's sequence, even after you leave the playground.

The dream tale

While you play, a story in eight chapters is told. Each chapter has a few lines and a small deed that leads you somewhere new:

  1. the playground
  2. the firefly
  3. keeping a melody
  4. the echo game
  5. the planets
  6. another Biome
  7. the Sound Lab
  8. all the balls at once

When a deed is done, its card rises over the stage.

The garden shows quietly at its foot where the tale stands, and so does the playground at the top. Progress is kept in mutabledreams/save/tale.txt. Delete that file to begin the tale again.

Mixed something up? Hold START and SELECT together for three seconds, anywhere, and every button is itself again.

Smoother

Since 1.1, the picture is drawn on a core of its own while the previous frame is shown, and button presses come through at once, even in the middle of a frame.

In the menu, the quick bar, the guide and the options, a held arrow keeps going and gets faster.

Language

Every version starts in English. The quick bar's last stone, DEUTSCH / ENGLISH, switches the whole interface, and your choice is saved.

The names of sound parameters, modules, Biomes and worlds stay in English, as on the FM-1. The translation lives in mutabledreams/lang/de.txt, where you can improve it.

USB-C audio

Plug a class-compliant USB sound card or a USB-C headphone adapter into the Brick, and the sound goes there. USB AUDIO appears, with the name of the device. Pull it out, and the sound returns to the Brick's own speaker without a crash. An empty file mutabledreams/usbaudio.off keeps the sound on the Brick.

Whether a particular device is recognised depends on Knulli. After each start, mutabledreams/log.txt lists every output the system sees, with USB ones marked "(USB)".

MIDI

A class-compliant USB keyboard or controller plays POLY when POLY is on. Otherwise MIDI channels 1–6 play the six tracks.

MIDI only goes in: Mutable Dreams sends no notes and no clock. When you unplug a keyboard, all of its notes stop.

Ideas for making music

A quiet evening

  1. Open Worlds and enter Kelp Cathedral from Deep Waters.
  2. Turn MIST up and FIREFLIES down a little.
  3. Press Y to wander. Every few minutes the garden glides into the next world.
Worlds: choose a family on the left and a world on the right; Y lets the garden wander on.

A rhythm of your own

  1. On the playground, go to the Wheel and set three rings, for example 3 in 8, 5 in 16 and 4 in 16.
  2. Choose the track with X, then keep the pattern with R2 + X.
  3. Open GRIDS in the Sound Lab to add kick, snare and hi-hat.
The Wheel: each ring is a track, its hits spread evenly over its steps.

A melody that stays

  1. Catch four or five notes with the Firefly.
  2. Listen for a few bars, then keep them as a sequence with R2 + X.
  3. Refine it in the Sequence with X, for example with chance and gate.
The Firefly: every glowing note caught joins the melody.

Voices in the garden

  1. Fetch a poem in the Archive.
  2. Send it to VOICES with A, and choose FRAGMENTS or GRAINS.
  3. Add a little SHIMMER, and the words float through the garden.
The archive: poems to fetch, then to send into the garden's voices.

Falling asleep

  1. Open Worlds and enter one of the Lullabies; it sets the sleep timer itself.
  2. Or set it yourself in the options: SLEEP TIMER 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes.
  3. Turn GLOW down. In the last minute the music fades out, and the screen rests dark until a button wakes it.
The options: sleep timer, repeat speed, buttons and more.

Playing live

  1. Switch on play mode: the FN switch on the Brick, or PLAY MODE in the quick bar (L2).
  2. The D-pad and A B X Y now play the Biome's scale; L1/R1 change the track.
  3. Let a sequence run with START and play over it; hold R2 for chords.
Play mode: the right side shows which button plays which tone.

On the go: Miyoo, RGB20S, phone

  1. Tap SELECT for the menu and pick a place; on a phone, use the touch buttons beside the stage.
  2. Tap L2 for the quick bar: PLAY MODE, LIGHT PICTURE and DEUTSCH / ENGLISH are at its end.
  3. To stop, tap SELECT, press UP once to reach Quit, and A. The Sound Lab and your settings are kept on the way out; to be sure of everything, hold R2 + SELECT for a second first.
The menu, opened with SELECT on handhelds: Quit is the last leaf on the right.

Saving and your files

  • Save everything: hold R2 + SELECT for one second.
  • mutabledreams/save/ holds your Biomes, the Sound Lab (lab.bin), the tale (tale.txt) and your settings (brick-prefs.txt: calm motion, light picture, the last toy and the language).
  • mutabledreams/samples/ takes your own WAV files, and samples/voices/ takes voices as Ogg or WAV.
  • An update never loses any of these.

Questions and help

There is no sound.

Press START: RESTING means the music is stopped. Also check the Brick's volume keys, and if a USB sound card is plugged in, check whether the sound goes there.

I am lost.

Hold B, and you are home. A short tap on SELECT tells you where you are.

The picture stutters.

Switch on LIGHT PICTURE in the quick bar. It also comes on by itself when the stage stays slow. The sound always comes before the picture.

How do I begin the tale again?

Delete mutabledreams/save/tale.txt.

I want everything back as new.

Rename the folder mutabledreams/save. On the next start everything is new, and your old state stays in the renamed folder.
